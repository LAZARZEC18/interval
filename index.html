        Questions

        Does the timer keep going if I switch apps or lock my phone?
        Yes. Interval works out the time from timestamps rather than counting ticks, so when you come back it jumps to exactly the right phase. While it runs it also asks the browser to keep your screen on. Some phones pause web audio while the screen is locked, so leave Interval open for beeps.
        Why can't I hear the beeps?
        Browsers only allow sound after you tap something on the page, so the first press of Start unlocks it. On an iPhone, check the silent switch. Open Sound in the app bar and press Test sound to check your volume.
        How do repeat groups work?
        In the builder, turn on Repeat a group and choose the first and last block to repeat, then how many times. Blocks before the group play once at the start and blocks after it play once at the end, which is handy for a warm-up and cool-down.
        What do Back and Skip do?
        Skip jumps to the next block. Back restarts the current block, or goes to the previous one if you press it in the first two seconds. You can also select any block in the timeline under the ring to jump straight to it.
        Where are my workouts and history stored?
        In your browser's storage on this device. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account. Clearing your browser data removes them.
        Does it work offline?
        Yes. Once the page has loaded, the timer, cues, builder and history all work without a connection.

        Sound and cues

        Ticks at 3, 2, 1 and a different tone for work, rest and done.
        Says each phase as it starts, plus "Halfway" and "Done".
